# 🇳🇿 New Zealand Sales & Export Data Analysis

An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** analyzing global sales, profits, item demand, and regional market distribution for New Zealand export operations across a 2010–2017 timeline.

## 📈 High-Level Metrics (KPI Overview)

* **Total Revenue (Sales):** $665 Billion
* **Total Profit:** $196 Billion
* **Total Units Sold:** 2 Billion units
* **Global Reach:** 185 Countries across 7 Regions
* **Product Catalog:** 12 Item Types

---

## 🔍 Key Dashboard Insights

### 1. 🌍 Sales Distribution by Region
* **Sub-Saharan Africa ($174bn)** and **Europe ($171bn)** are the top two revenue-generating markets, accounting for more than half of overall total sales.
* **Asia ($97bn)**, **Middle East & North Africa ($83bn)**, and **Central America ($72bn)** represent strong secondary export markets.
* **North America ($15bn)** records the lowest total sales among the seven regions analyzed.

### 2. 📦 Top Performing Categories (Sales by Item Type)
* **Household ($139bn)** and **Office Supplies ($135bn)** drive the highest revenue volume.
* **Cosmetics ($91bn)** and **Meat ($88bn)** show robust demand.
* Lower revenue contributors include **Beverages ($10bn)** and **Fruits ($2bn)**.

### 3. 🛒 Sales Channel Breakdown
* Sales are split relatively evenly between **Offline** and **Online** channels, demonstrating a balanced multi-channel distribution strategy.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX measures, and visual design.
